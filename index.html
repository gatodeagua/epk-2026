About

We like our rock heavy, our grooves hypnotic and our psychedelia a little twisted. Somewhere between desert heat, fuzz-drenched riffs and the darkest corners of Barcelona, SLØTHS found their sound. No genre rules, no clean-cut edges — just four musicians chasing the point where melody gets dirty and things start to unravel.

Born in London and raised in Barcelona’s underground, we released Terraces in 2026: a nine-track trip through guilt, desire and transcendence. But records only tell half the story. The rest happens live, where riffs stretch, grooves lock in and the atmosphere takes over.

We’re here to play loud, get lost and take the room with us. Promoters, bookers, curious listeners — if you like your rock with a little grit and a psychedelic streak, you know what to do.

Book a show. Come down. See what happens.
